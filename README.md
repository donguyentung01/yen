# yên

A Vietnamese meditation app for people who open it because something happened.

Most meditation apps ask you to pick a category — sleep, focus, breathing — as
though you arrived with a plan. Yên assumes you didn't. You open it at 2am
because you can't sleep, or after a breakup, or on a morning you can't face. So
the first thing it asks is simply: **Ê, nay sao rồi?**

## How it works

Pick how you feel. That's the whole first screen.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Khó ngủ 🌙** | Nằm hoài không ngủ được |
| **Stress 😵‍💫** | Công việc, cuộc sống |
| **Thất tình 💔** | Chia tay, nhớ, buồn |
| **Bắt đầu ngày mới ☀️** | Trước khi đi học, đi làm |
| **Hàng ngày 🙂** | Nay không có gì đặc biệt |

Each one opens with a line that acknowledges where you are before offering
anything — *"Ừ thì toang. Nhưng mà ổn thôi."* — and then three things to listen
to:

- **Thở** — about 5 minutes. Breath counting, barely any words. For when you
  need something to do with the next few minutes.
- **Thiền** — 10 to 20 minutes. A slower voice, no demand that you feel better
  by the end.
- **Truyện** — 20 to 30 minutes. A story read aloud. Not an exercise; the
  calm comes from being told something.

You get one of each per day, and they rotate. No library to browse, no decisions
beyond the first one.

## What it won't do

**It won't tell you you'll be fine.** No forced positivity, no "bạn sẽ ổn thôi"
in the middle of a bad night. The writing acknowledges first and never promises
a fix.

**It won't punish you for disappearing.** There's no streak to break. Hành trình
counts the time you've spent and the days you showed up, and both only ever go
up — because an app for people at their lowest should never greet them with
what they lost.

**It won't ask you to write anything down.** No journalling, no mood log, no
prompts. You listen, and that's it.

**It won't ask who you are.** No account, no login, no email. Your listening
history stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

## Why Vietnamese, properly

Not translated wellness copy. The writing is Gen Z Vietnamese as actually
spoken — *toang*, *khum*, *ẻm* — and the imagery is local: cánh đồng, mùi lúa
mới, tiếng chuông chùa, mưa mái tôn, rather than the beaches and forests every
Western meditation app reaches for.

Everything is written original, in Vietnamese, from scratch.

## Status

In development. Free, with no ads and no premium tier.
